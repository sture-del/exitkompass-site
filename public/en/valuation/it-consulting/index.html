What is an IT consulting company worth?

Small and medium-sized Nordic companies in this industry sell on average for around 7.1x EBITDA, or about 0.8x revenue. The exact value of an IT consulting company depends on size, profitability and how dependent the business is on its owner. Here is the starting point and what moves the value.

What raises the value?

  • Framework agreements and long assignments with several clients.
  • High utilisation and employed consultants rather than subcontractors.
  • A specialisation that is hard to replace, such as security, data or a specific platform.

What lowers the value?

  • The owner personally selling and delivering the largest assignments.
  • Heavy dependence on one client or one framework agreement.
  • High staff turnover or many low-margin subcontractors.

Who are the buyers?

Buyers are larger consulting groups, serial acquirers and private equity building platforms. Companies with their own products or managed-service contracts get higher multiples.

Examples of active Nordic acquirers in the industry: Röko, Storskogen.

Frequently asked questions

Which multiple is used for an IT consulting company?

The starting point is around 7.1x EBITDA for a mid-sized company, with higher multiples for larger companies and lower for smaller ones. The company's own characteristics can move the multiple several steps up or down.

Can you value on revenue instead?

Revenue multiples (here around 0.8x) are used mainly when profitability is low or temporarily depressed. Exitkompass blends EBITDA, EBIT and revenue multiples depending on profitability.
