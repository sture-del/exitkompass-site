The services

  • Report: an indicative valuation and exit-readiness analysis of the company as a PDF, reviewed by a senior adviser.

  • The report is based on the company's annual reports, the information and documents you provide and publicly available market data.

Who the service is for

The service is aimed at companies and business owners. The person ordering confirms that they may represent the company or its owners and provide the information needed.

Price and payment

Prices are shown on the website. No payment is taken when you order. An invoice is sent with the report, payable within 10 days. Late payment interest is charged under applicable law.

Business customers outside Sweden are invoiced without Swedish VAT under the reverse-charge rules.

Delivery

The report is normally delivered within 1–2 working days after we have received the information needed. If something is missing we contact you, and the delivery time runs from when the material is complete. The review is scheduled with you.

Right of withdrawal

The service is sold to businesses, so consumer distance-selling rules normally do not apply. If you nevertheless order as a consumer, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal. By ordering you ask us to start work immediately, and once the report has been delivered the right of withdrawal has expired. If you cancel before delivery, you pay for the work already done.

Nature of the report and liability

The valuation is indicative. It is based on information from you and public sources, which we do not fully verify. The report is not an audit, a fairness opinion or tax or legal advice and is not intended for purposes such as divorce settlements, inheritance, tax or disputes. What a buyer actually pays depends on market conditions, the buyer, financing and deal terms.

Our liability for errors in a delivered service is limited to the amount you paid for it. We are not liable for indirect losses, such as lost profit, or for errors caused by incorrect information from you.

Complaints

If the report contains errors, you must tell us within 14 days of delivery. We correct factual errors free of charge.

Confidentiality and use of the report

We treat your information and documents confidentially, see the privacy policy. You may use the report for your own purposes and share it with your advisers, lenders and potential buyers. The structure and other content of the report belong to Adsco Capital Partners.

Governing law and disputes

Swedish law applies. Disputes are settled by the general courts with the Stockholm District Court as the first instance. Consumers keep the protection of mandatory rules in their country of residence.
