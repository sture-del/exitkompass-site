Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy describes how Adsco Capital Partners processes personal data when you use Exitkompass at exitkompass.se, ask the question bot or order a report.

Data controller

Adsco Capital Partners
Storgatan 7, 1st floor
SE-114 44 Stockholm
Sweden
Email: kontakt@exitkompass.se

What data we process

  • Company registration numbers and company information you look up. This is public information from the Swedish Companies Registration Office or the Brønnøysund Register Centre.
  • Figures and answers you enter, such as the owner's salary, customer concentration and debt.
  • Documents you upload, such as annual reports, budgets, business plans and monthly reports.
  • Name, email, phone number and company when you order a report or a meeting, or ask for a copy of the result or a reminder.
  • Questions you type to the question bot.
  • Technical statistics: which steps of the tool are used, linked to a random id that only applies in the current browser tab, and visit statistics from Cloudflare Web Analytics without cookies.

Purposes and legal basis

  • To produce your valuation and deliver an ordered report (contract).
  • To send a copy of the result or a reminder to update the valuation, when you have asked for it (consent). You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • To answer questions, improve the tool and follow how it is used (legitimate interest).
  • To meet bookkeeping rules for orders and invoices (legal obligation).

Where data is stored and who receives it

Data and documents are stored in Adsco Capital Partners' Google Workspace. The website runs on Cloudflare. Google and Cloudflare are processors and may only process the data on our behalf. They may process data outside the EU/EEA under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

We never sell personal data and do not share your data or documents with buyers or anyone else without your explicit approval.

How long data is kept

  • Uploaded documents and valuation data are kept as long as needed for the assignment and are normally deleted no later than 24 months after the last contact, or earlier if you ask.
  • Orders and invoice records are kept for seven years under the Swedish Bookkeeping Act.
  • Email addresses for copies and reminders are kept until you unsubscribe or the reminder has been sent.
  • Step statistics and questions to the question bot are kept for at most 24 months.

Cookies and browser storage

Exitkompass uses no tracking or marketing cookies. Browser storage is used to remember your language and the random id that counts the steps in the tool. Cloudflare Web Analytics works without cookies.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to receive data you have provided in a machine-readable format. Contact us at kontakt@exitkompass.se.

If you are unhappy with how we process your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se) or the supervisory authority where you live.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
